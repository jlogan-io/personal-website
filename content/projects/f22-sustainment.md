As lead Program Manager for Northrop Grumman's F-22 repairs line, I ran a
multi-year, multimillion-dollar effort to maintain and repair advanced avionics
— specifically software-defined radios and power amplifier modules — for
Lockheed Martin and the U.S. Air Force. The role combined technical program
delivery, contract negotiation and direct profit-and-loss accountability, and
required maintaining a security clearance throughout.

## Running the line

I directed execution of the software-defined radio and power amplifier module
repairs, aligning engineering, production and logistics across both internal
teams and external partners. Alongside the repair work itself, the program
carried full lifecycle management of several new technical development efforts
— capabilities that did not exist yet and had to be built while the existing
line kept delivering.

## The customer relationship

I served as the primary interface with Lockheed Martin, owning technical
delivery and weekly performance reporting, and worked directly with customers
across several external agencies to manage expectations and report status and
cost. The program also supported multiple program-level audits from outside
agencies.

## Winning the follow-on

Using past actuals and hands-on knowledge of what the work genuinely cost, I
built the estimates, generated the proposal and negotiated directly with the
customer to win a **$25M five-year follow-on repair contract** — securing
long-term revenue and materially expanding the program's strategic footprint.

## Reporting up

Earned Value Management, schedule performance and program profitability were
reported directly to Northrop Grumman VP leadership. Accurate forecasting was
the job: on a repair line, an optimistic schedule is a cost problem two
quarters later.

## Leading the teams

The program ran on diverse engineering skill sets pulled across disciplines. I
led and managed those teams to hit repair timelines, reduce operational
bottlenecks and keep output quality consistent across a multi-year effort.

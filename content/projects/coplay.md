An AI assistant for PMOs, built and run in production — then evaluated against
its own live telemetry for the M.S. Engineering Leadership & Management
capstone (EML-595). Retrieval grounded in the program's own corpus: 24,500
documents across 14 portfolios.

- Owned the application and its operating environment — from first commit through general availability, 37 modular workflow capabilities.
- 94.3% of tracked action items captured automatically; status-report turnaround at a 7.7-minute median.
- Evaluation as the deliverable: predefined KPIs, a derived time-savings model, and a faithfulness audit grading output against its sources.
